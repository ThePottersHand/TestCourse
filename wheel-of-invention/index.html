A blueprint of human ingenuity

The Wheel of Invention

Fifteen breakthroughs across 5,500 years, from the first wheel to thinking machines.

c.BCE

0:00
  1. c. 3500 BCE, Mesopotamia: the wheel. First it spun clay, then it carried the world.
  2. c. 250 BCE, Syracuse: Archimedes' screw, which lifts water uphill.
  3. 1088, Song dynasty China: the magnetic compass, called the south-pointing needle.
  4. 1440, Mainz: Gutenberg's printing press, 3,600 pages a day.
  5. 1608, Middelburg: the telescope. Galileo turned one on the Moon a year later.
  6. 1656, The Hague: Huygens' pendulum clock.
  7. 1769, Glasgow: James Watt's steam engine.
  8. 1844, Washington to Baltimore: Morse's telegraph sends "What hath God wrought".
  9. 1879, Menlo Park: Edison's light bulb.
  10. 1886, Mannheim: Benz's automobile.
  11. 1903, Kitty Hawk: the Wright brothers' airplane flies for 12 seconds.
  12. 1928, London: Fleming's penicillin.
  13. 1969, Florida: the Saturn V launches Apollo 11 to the Moon.
  14. 1977: the personal computer arrives in homes.
  15. 1989, CERN: Tim Berners-Lee proposes the World Wide Web.
  16. Today: artificial intelligence, trained on everything we have written, drawn and built.
